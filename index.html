About the Spartans

Spartans Cricket Club is an Eastvale, California cricket club competing in the East Vale Premier League of the Sands Cricket Association. Five championships, a title hat-trick and a squad that plays for each other.

Our story

The Spartans bring together cricketers who share one goal: playing hard, competitive cricket the right way. The five stars on our crest stand for the standard we set ourselves every time we take the field.

Our first championships came in 2022, when the Spartans won the Summer League and then made it back-to-back in the Winter League 2022–23. Since then the club has become one of the most consistent sides in the league, finishing top of the table four times, reaching one more final as runners-up and collecting five titles in all.

Home is Harada Heritage Park in Eastvale, where match days are as much about the team as the result, from the first ball of the season to the barbecue after the last.

The captain

Leading from the front

Since taking the armband in 2022, Harsha Gubbi Chandrashekar has captained the Spartans to all five of the club’s championships, from that first Summer League title to the EPL – Div 1 hat-trick. Calm under pressure and relentless in his standards, he has a knack for turning tight finishes into team triumphs. Behind the stumps and in the huddle, his belief is contagious, lifting every player to perform beyond themselves. More than a captain, he is the heartbeat of the Spartans: first to celebrate a teammate, and never one to settle for second best.
