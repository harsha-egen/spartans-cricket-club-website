The players driving the Spartans’ 2026 season

By Spartans Cricket Club · Nov 10, 2026 · 2 min read

Every Spartans season has its headline numbers, but the 2026 campaign has been shaped by a handful of players who keep turning up in the big moments. Using the club’s scorecards from EPL Div. 1 (4.0) 2026, here are the players driving the side, and the performances that define their seasons.

Jaydev Natavar Chotalia: the all-round match-winner

No Spartan has influenced more games. Jaydev leads the run charts with 202 runs at a strike rate of almost 157, and he has eight wickets on top. His best day with the ball came against OC Fighters, when he took 5 for 10 to seal a 38-run win. With the bat, his 63 off 35 balls against Kings cc finished a chase of 118 in just 12.2 overs, after he had already taken 3 for 21 in the same game. He also struck 43 off 25 balls to set up the 20-run win over Boltz, and he arrives at the Knights game with 90 runs in his last three innings.

Dweep Jhaveri: the control in the middle

Dweep is the leading wicket-taker with 12, and he has done it while conceding just 3.94 runs an over, the best economy rate of any Spartan to bowl six overs or more. The figures tell the story of his reliability: four overs for 5 runs against Rebels cc, 3 for 12 against Rams and three wickets against Boltz. He has chipped in with the bat too, top-scoring with 36 off 31 balls against Corona Lions.

Abhinandan Rissam: the steady top order

Abhinandan has 114 runs, the second-most in the squad. His 66 off 57 balls against Rebels cc was the highest score of the season so far, and his unbeaten 29 off 20 balls finished the 9-wicket win over Ahir Tigers. He has contributed with the ball as well, taking 3 for 17 in the 76-run win over La Jaguars.

Ripal Patel: the wicket at the right time

Ripal has six wickets and some of the most important spells of the year. His 4 for 20 held La Zalmi to 82/9 and set up an eight-wicket win, and his 2 for 10 against Ahir Tigers kept the opposition to 45/7 in a ten-over game. His caught-and-bowled stunner is one of the featured clips on Spartans TV.

Chirag Ahir and Vivek Desai: the strike bowlers

Two of the best bowling performances of the season came from these two. Chirag Ahir took 5 for 25 against Rams to bowl them out for 122, and has six wickets in total. Vivek Desai’s 4 for 7 from four overs against Kings cc was one of the most economical spells of the year, part of his seven wickets.

Strength in depth

Behind them is a deep supporting cast. Kanishk Hari (107 runs) and Sameep Kulkarni (102 runs) have both passed a hundred runs, Mrunal Ketan Patel has 99 including a 51 against OC Fighters, and Jash Chhavi Jain has been the second most economical bowler at 4.27 an over. With 35 players in the squad, the Spartans rarely depend on one performer, and that depth has been the foundation of five championships.
