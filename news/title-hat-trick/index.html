Three in a row: inside the Spartans’ title hat-trick

By Spartans Cricket Club · Nov 10, 2026 · 3 min read

Between January 2025 and June 2026 the Spartans won three consecutive championships in the East Vale Premier League: EPL – Div 1 (1.0) [2024–25], EPL – Div 1 (2.0) [2025] and EPL – Div 1 (3.0) [2025–26]. Across those three seasons they played 42 matches, won 31, lost 9 and had 2 washed out. They reached three finals and won all three: by 8 runs, by 41 runs and by 10 wickets. This is the story of that run.

Title one: EPL – Div 1 (1.0) [2024–25]

The first campaign opened on January 5, 2025 with a 66-run win over Zen’s cc, and the Spartans won five of their first six games. March was the test. They lost to California Rhinos by one wicket, to Kings cc by three wickets and to Oc Knights by five wickets, all within four weeks. What followed defined the season: six straight wins to the finish line, including a 90-run demolition of La United and a 59-run win over Corona Lions.

The semi-final against Silverbacks cc was the tightest game of the year. Chasing 159, the Spartans got there with two balls to spare and three wickets in hand. In the final on June 15, 2025 they posted 162/9 against La kings and defended it, holding their opponents to 154/6 to win by eight runs. Final record: 11 wins and 3 defeats.

Title two: EPL – Div 1 (2.0) [2025]

The second season, played from July to December 2025, showed what this batting line-up could do. The Spartans opened with a 77-run win over Warriors, edged Fateh cc by two runs and then piled up 236/3 against Mavericks, the highest total of the hat-trick, winning by 82 runs. They also scored 206/4 against La kings.

There were stumbles. On November 2 they lost twice in a day, to Ahir Tigers by two wickets and to Kings cc by 25 runs, and the game against Rebels cc was washed out. The knockouts were ruthless, though. In the semi-final Fateh cc were bowled out for 47 and the Spartans knocked off the runs without losing a wicket in 5.1 overs. In the final on December 7, 2025, Mrunal Ketan Patel’s century, captured on Spartans TV, drove the side to 173/6, and California Rhinos were bowled out for 132. Final record: 10 wins, 3 defeats and 1 no result.

Title three: EPL – Div 1 (3.0) [2025–26]

The third title came from January to June 2026. The season started with an 8-run win over Oc Knights, and there were narrow defeats to Shaheen cc, Fateh cc (by two runs) and Ahir Tigers along the way. But the big wins kept coming: 195/2 against Rebels cc, a 92-run victory over Avians, and a 10-wicket chase against Corona Lions completed in 11.2 overs.

The finish was the most dominant of all. Corona Lions were beaten by 61 runs in the semi-final, and in the final on June 7, 2026 Silverbacks cc made 129/8 before the Spartans chased it down without losing a wicket, reaching 130/0 in 15.2 overs. Final record: 10 wins, 3 defeats and 1 no result.

What made the run possible

Three patterns stand out. First, the Spartans never let a bad week become a bad season: every title run included defeats, and none turned into a slump. Second, they produced their best cricket in knockouts, winning six knockout matches across the three seasons. Third, they posted big totals when it mattered, with scores of 236, 206, 195 and 192 across the run. Add the two titles of 2022 and 2022–23 and the club now has five championships, all under captain Harsha Gubbi Chandrashekar.
