Six wins, two washouts and a chasing machine: the Spartans’ 2026 season so far

By Spartans Cricket Club · Nov 10, 2026 · 3 min read

The Spartans head into the last scheduled league match of the EPL Div. 1 (4.0) 2026 season with six wins, three defeats and two washouts from eleven fixtures. That is a 67% win rate from completed games, built on a batting line-up that keeps finding a match-winner and a bowling attack that rarely leaks runs. Here is how the season has unfolded, and what it tells us before the November 15 meeting with Knights.

A season of two halves

The campaign started with a setback. On July 12 Abhinandan Rissam top-scored with 66 off 57 balls to lift the Spartans to 130/7, and Dweep Jhaveri conceded just 5 runs from his four overs, but Rebels cc still reached 131/3 with nine balls to spare. The response was immediate. Ripal Patel took 4 for 20 as La Zalmi were held to 82/9, and the Spartans finished that chase in 11 overs. A week later Mrunal Ketan Patel made 51 off 40 balls and Jaydev Natavar Chotalia took 5 for 10 to beat OC Fighters by 38 runs.

August 23 brought a 20-run win over Boltz after Jaydev struck 43 off 25 balls in a total of 161/9. September was mixed: Chirag Ahir’s 5 for 25 bowled Rams out for 122, only for the chase to fall seven runs short, and Corona Lions chased down 134 despite 36 from Dweep Jhaveri. In between, Kanishk Hari’s 37 off 21 balls and Gunjan Patel’s 30 set up a 76-run thrashing of La Jaguars.

October belonged to the Spartans. Vivek Desai took 4 for 7 and Jaydev 3 for 21 as Kings cc were bowled out for 117, and Jaydev then hit 63 off 35 balls to win by eight wickets in 12.2 overs. Against Ahir Tigers, Ripal Patel’s 2 for 10 restricted the opposition to 45/7 in a ten-over game, and Abhinandan Rissam’s unbeaten 29 off 20 balls finished it in 5.4 overs. Rain washed out both the October 11 game against Silverbacks cc and the November 1 meeting with Fateh cc.

The batting-first lesson

The numbers tell a clear story. Chasing, the Spartans are 3–1, and the only miss was that seven-run defeat to Rams. Batting first they are 3–2, and the split is about the size of the total. Scores of 161, 170 and 177 were all defended comfortably. Both defeats came after posting 130 and 134. When the Spartans set a target above 150 this season, they have not lost.

Who is scoring the runs

Jaydev Natavar Chotalia leads the way with 202 runs at a strike rate of almost 157, and he is also in the best recent form with 90 runs across the last three matches. Abhinandan Rissam (114), Kanishk Hari (107) and Sameep Kulkarni (102) have all passed a hundred runs, with Mrunal Ketan Patel just behind on 99. That depth matters: five different batters have top-scored in a win.

Who is taking the wickets

Dweep Jhaveri has been the anchor of the attack with 12 wickets at an economy rate of 3.94, the best of any Spartan who has bowled at least six overs. Jaydev Natavar Chotalia has eight wickets to go with his runs, Vivek Desai seven, and Ripal Patel and Chirag Ahir six each. Jash Chhavi Jain has been the next most economical at 4.27 an over.

Next up: Knights

The Spartans meet Knights on November 15 at 4:30 PM on Field # 5. Since 2025 the teams have met three times, with the Spartans winning two: by 8 runs in January 2026 and by 35 runs in August 2025. Knights, then listed as Oc Knights, won the first meeting by five wickets in March 2025. With Jaydev in form and Dweep controlling the middle overs, the Spartans go in as the side to beat. If they bat first, the target is simple: get past 150.
